PR #56: Optional strict phonetic-quality gate for unattended lecture QA. When --require-calibrated-phoneme is supplied with --phoneme-manifest and --phoneme-audio-dir, the pipeline recalibrates two direct audio judges against genuine labeled WAVs on each run, then checks every eligible y-value source risk in the lesson. Failed calibration, audible YAY or UNKNOWN prevents full-lecture ASR and technical clearance. A passing narrow result is independently cross-checked against narration and WAV hashes, and saved to the new external QA packet. Without the option, legacy autonomous ASR flow remains unchanged and does not claim phonetic accuracy. No manual approval gate or publisher action; this does not establish correctness of all words, figures or highlighted elements.
