PR #60 — Portable waveform texture measurement: per 10/20/40 ms window, calculate waveform sign-change fraction (zero crossings) and mean absolute signed PCM step, alongside RMS, silence and clipping checks. These features can be informative about noise/voicing and abrupt consonants but **cannot independently classify why vs yay or any correct term**. The input WAV is SHA-checked before and after analysis, with no NumPy, SciPy, Whisper or external API. Absolute feature differences between two re-synthesized recordings must not be equated to a verified pronunciation mistake.
