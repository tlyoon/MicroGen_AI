## Integrated production changes

- `microgen_batch.wait_for_gemini_circuit`: bounded external capacity pause (default **900 seconds**, valid explicit range **60–7200 seconds**) and no endless wait for an unavailable Gemini service. The stage runner also caps repeated circuit-reopen cycles at two.
- `microgen_batch.run_subprocess`: a source/authorization-pinned paid stage whose whole-process result is uncertain **must not be relaunched** as a retry. The existing checkpoint, individual request evidence and original provider scope are preserved. Inexpensive or clearly nonbillable transient stages continue to use bounded backoff/retries.
- `microgen_recovery_registry_v6.json` registers the finite circuit pause, repeated outage and uncertain paid whole-stage effect, with distinct **PAUSED_EXTERNAL_DEPENDENCY** and **UNRESOLVED_EXTERNAL_EFFECT_HOLD** dispositions. `microgen_recovery_registry.classify_runtime_error` recognizes parent-runner-generated failures in addition to child tracebacks.
- The normal `microgen_batch.process_one` exception handler saves these dispositions and next eligible operation atomically with its standard checkpoint. It does not mark QA accepted and does not issue another grant.
- Main's normal batch target loop continues independent, already authorized work after one target fails if `--fail-fast` was not selected, retaining a nonzero aggregate status.
