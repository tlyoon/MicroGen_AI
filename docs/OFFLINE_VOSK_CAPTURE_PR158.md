# Optional offline Vosk transcription adapter (PR #158)

MicroGen_AI now includes `microgen_offline_vosk_capture.py`, which converts source-hashed comparison WAVs to bounded PCM16 and invokes a **locally installed** Vosk model to produce word-level recognition timestamps and confidence values. This module makes no network requests, does not download a model, and is imported lazily so the ordinary CI tests do not require Vosk or its model weights.

HP-only local development installation: `vosk==0.3.45` in an isolated AppData virtual environment and the official English small model `vosk-model-small-en-us-0.15` in a separate AppData model directory. ZIP SHA-256 `30f26242c4eb449f948e42cb302dd7a686cb29a3423a8367f99ff41780942498`. The model is not committed or redistributed with the MIT-licensed MicroGen_AI repository. Official project and license information: https://alphacephei.com/vosk/ and https://alphacephei.com/vosk/models .

The real Thomas Calculus §2.5 reproduction processed seven SHA-bound WAVs (three original scene extracts and four historical QA trials), saving `2_5_offline_vosk_capture_PR158.json` under HP AppData. Running the `microgen_offline_pronunciation_contrast.py` evaluator on this new output reproduced the findings from PR #157: slide 6 contains three high-confidence legacy 'circa' recognitions and a trial with 'see/sea/see'; slide 10 shows an additional recognized 'why' in both trials; slide 5 remains inconclusive.

**This is not a forced aligner and is not independent speech ground truth.** Vosk word timestamps are recognition-derived suggestions. A model-reported confidence of 1.0 is not certification of correct phonetics. The adapter cannot verify whether a mathematical letter 'c' or 'y' was truly spoken as intended without additional grounded review. It provides candidate word times for conservative source-PDF visual matching only, and every generated report declares `publication_allowed=false`. No source media, videos or legacy WAVs were replaced.
