PR #57 — Conservative selection of source-verified contrastive phonetic model pairs. Reads candidate direct audio reports (not normalized ASR), verifies actual WAV hashes, recorded model identities, yes/no labels against the known defective and corrected real clips, blind-judgment metadata, classification and aggregate consistency. Only live-observed reports can qualify; unknown/outage/altered/offline results produce NO_QUALIFIED_AUDIO_PAIR. Deterministic selection and bounded candidate set. No manual approval gate, no reproduction of raw audio/credentials, no publication. Even qualification is restricted to two examples and cannot justify general phonetic-accuracy claims.
